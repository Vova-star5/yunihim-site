# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS for now (one self-contained page per design variant). Later the chosen variant will be ported to WordPress, so markup should stay plain and section-based, easy to split into theme templates.

## Users

B2B buyers responsible for supplies at laundries (прачечные), hotels (гостиницы), dry cleaners (химчистки) and consumer-service shops (дома быта). They come to find a reliable producer of washing/cleaning products, check the range and formats, and request a price list or call. Secondary: companies looking for contract manufacturing under their own brand, and buyers of soap base.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for the new household-chemicals direction of ООО «ЮниХим-Про». It states what the company produces and for whom, and collects requests (price list, call back). Success: a visitor understands within seconds that this is a producer serving their type of business and leaves a request or calls.

## Positioning

Own production in Tuchkovo, Moscow Oblast (not a reseller). Besides finished products, the company offers wholesale supply, contract manufacturing (private label) and supply of soap base.

## Operating Context

- Ready products now: liquid soap (several kinds), laundry gel (several scents), fabric conditioner (several scents), dishwashing liquid (several scents).
- Coming later: shower gels, shampoos and more. The products block must grow by adding cards without a redesign; a full catalog may follow when the range reaches 15-20 items.
- No cart, no prices on the site: the action is a request (form) or a phone call.

## Product Principles

1. Producer, not shop: speak to businesses about supply, formats and reliability, never retail-style.
2. Show the actual products and how they are used in each client's work, not generic cleanliness imagery.
3. One clear action everywhere: request a price list / call.
4. Built to grow: new products slot in without redesign.
