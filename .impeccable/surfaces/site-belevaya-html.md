Scope: one-page landing, variant A of three. Mode: Persuade. Audience: B2B supply buyers at laundries, hotels, dry cleaners, домы быта. Action: request price list or call. Copy: content.md. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented figures; placeholders labeled).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a hotel linen room. Products sit as folded stacks on a shelf, each with a sewn-in woven tag. It refuses the category default of a bottle-on-white hero over rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cotton-white ground. Blue-white ticking stripe (#24439A) owns whole bands. Towel greys (#DCE1E6). Woven tags are the only saturated objects: red #D8392E reserved for the primary action and the active state, with yellow #F1BE2C, green #2E8B4F and blue as product tags. Dashed machine stitching serves as seams and dividers, and one continuous seam runs from the header to the form. Condensed label capitals (Alumni Sans) set the tags and display; Onest sets the body; numbers are tabular.

STORY: The buyer recognises their own linen room. Four products sit as stacks, and each tag gives scents and volumes. Then come who it serves, three ways to cooperate and four steps to start. The page ends on a form styled as a tag to fill in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin shelf-label header holds the logo placeholder, nav, phone and a red tag button. A ticking ribbon spans about 60% of the width and carries the condensed headline at roughly 88px. Beneath it a full-width shelf holds four folded stacks of different heights, built from CSS layers with stitched edges, each with a hanging woven tag. The primary action is a red tag "Получить прайс" pinned at the ribbon's end.

SIGNATURE: Hover or focus on a stack lifts it off the shelf and flips its tag to reveal scents and volumes.

FORM: Hotel linen room / housekeeping cart, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 2c7ef929. Raises: continuous seam (from the provenance ribbon), tabular numerals (from datamatics), red reserved for the active state only (from raku).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
