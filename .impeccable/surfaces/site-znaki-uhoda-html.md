## Direction contract

THESIS: The site speaks the care-label language laundries and dry cleaners read every day. The page is built as a sewn-in care label, and each product is a "regime" with its own symbol row. It refuses generic cleanliness imagery such as bubbles, droplets and green leaves.

OWN-WORLD: Satin label white #F6F6F3 with a faint satin sheen and black print #111111. Cobalt #1F3FBF is the single committed accent and owns a full label band. Grey #8A8F96 is secondary. Care symbols are authored SVG in one 1.75 stroke: a washtub with temperature, triangle, iron, circle-P, tumble square. The page uses label anatomy throughout: sewn top fold, stitch line, cut ribbon ends. Commissioner sets the printed-label voice (condensed and wide). JetBrains Mono appears only for codes, temperatures and volumes.

STORY: The buyer meets ЮниХим as a label. Each product label shows its regime symbols, scents and volumes. "Для кого" reads as four regimes, one per kind of business. Then come the three cooperation formats, the steps, and a request form laid out as a label to fill in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left, a huge vertical care label (satin white, about 440px wide) is sewn in at the top edge and runs off the bottom of the viewport. It holds ЮниХим, "Сделано в Тучково, Московская обл.", and rows of symbols. On the right sits the headline at about 76px in condensed black, the subline, a cobalt "Получить прайс" button and the phone. Under the headline, four mini labels give the products.

SIGNATURE: Hover or focus on a product lights its matching symbols on the big label in cobalt, a cross-reference between product and regime.

FORM: Care-label notation (ISO 3758 symbols, satin labels), position 1 of 7; IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed key 2c7ef929.
