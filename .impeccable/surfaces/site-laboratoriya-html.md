Scope: one-page landing, variant C of three (it replaces the rejected «Справочник», which the user called kindergarten-like). Mode: Persuade. Audience: B2B supply buyers at laundries, hotels, dry cleaners and домы быта. Action: request a price list or call. Copy: content.md. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented figures; placeholders labeled). The register must be adult, restrained and serious: no rainbow, no toy shapes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the technologist's lab journal on graph paper. It proves "we make the formulas ourselves" by showing measured glassware and batch records. It refuses the category default of bubbles, leaves, droplets and icon cards, and it refuses colourful playfulness.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground: cool lab white #F7F8F6 with a fine millimetre grid (1mm hairlines #E3E8EA, 5mm lines #D3DADE). The grid is used only in hero and journal areas, never everywhere.
- Ink and accent: graphite #1A1E22. One reagent accent is bottle-glass green #0E6B55; amber #B7791F appears only in tiny measurement marks.
- Drawing: glassware (graduated cylinder, beaker, flask, 5 L canister) is fine 1.25px line SVG with graduation ticks and leader-line annotations.
- Product records: batch cards with ruled fields, in the manner of a laboratory journal.
- Type: Golos Text 400–700 for headings and body. Roboto Mono only for measurements, volumes and batch fields.

STORY: The buyer sees a producer with a lab, not a shop. Each product reads as a journal entry: purpose, scents, volumes, label status. "Для кого" is a clean ruled table of object → products. The formats and why-us come next, then a four-step "Методика", and a request form styled as a journal entry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Seven left columns hold the headline at about 80px in graphite, the subline, a green «Получить прайс» button and the phone. Five right columns hold a precise line drawing on graph paper: four vessels (one per product) at different fill levels, each with graduations and a leader line to a small mono label with the product name and volume.

SIGNATURE: Hover or focus on a product, in the hero labels or the journal, raises the liquid in its vessel along the graduations, with an exponential ease-out of about 600ms. Reduced-motion users get static levels.

FORM: Technologist's lab journal (candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list), chosen by the user from the safer re-roll; seed key 2c7ef929.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
